# DU Data Scientist

* With little additional work, you can get an additional diploma in Data Science
* This is a [_Diplôme universitaire_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipl%C3%B4me_universitaire), not a Bachelor/Master degree! It is not internationally recognized!
* You just need to take one extra course and exam in _Deep Learning_
* However, you will need to pay 450 € for enrollment
* You should decide until around 17.11.
* Responsible: [Prof. Julien Donini](https://imapp.eu/teacher/prof-julien-donini/)
* More information on the university's [website](https://www.uca.fr/formation/nos-formations/catalogue-des-formations/du-data-scientist)
* Obtaining the actual certificate is difficult:
  * It takes \~1 year to become available
  * You need to pick it up in person or jump through some hoops…
  * > Complete the [application form](https://eupi.uca.fr/medias/fichier/demande-envoi-diplome-etranger_1552485695172-pdf?ID_FICHE=63040\&INLINE=FALSE) here and return it to them, together with a copy of a valid identity document, a self-addressed A4 envelope, and a postage stamp for a 100-gram registered parcel or international reply coupon. The French embassy in your country of residence will contact you to let you know when it arrives and how to collect it.
